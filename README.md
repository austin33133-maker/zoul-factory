## About Spartifexon / 关于 Spartifexon

**Spartifexon** is a global OEM & ODM manufacturer with **17 years of
heritage** in consumer electronics, focused on four product lines: rapid
chargers, high-speed data cables, AI smart glasses and wireless CarPlay /
Android Auto adapters.

**Spartifexon** 是一家拥有 **17 年** 消费电子积淀的全球 OEM & ODM 制造商，
聚焦四大产品线：快充电源、高速数据线、AI 智能眼镜、无线 CarPlay / Android
Auto 适配器。

## Why Spartifexon / 为什么选择我们

- **17 Years of Manufacturing Heritage** — Mature supply chain and
  end-to-end quality control.
  **17 年制造积淀** — 成熟供应链与全流程品控。
- **Full-Stack Capability** — ID and structural design, PCBA, firmware,
  mobile app, cloud and mass production handled in-house.
  **全栈能力** — ID 与结构设计、PCBA、固件、App、云端、量产一站式完成。
- **Certified Quality** — CE · FCC · RoHS · UKCA · USB-IF and other
  regional certifications appropriate to each product line.
  **品质认证** — CE · FCC · RoHS · UKCA · USB-IF 等区域认证按品类齐全。
- **OEM & ODM Flexibility** — Logo, packaging, firmware, colourways,
  components and MOQ all configurable.
  **OEM & ODM 灵活定制** — LOGO、包装、固件、配色、元器件与起订量均可定制。
