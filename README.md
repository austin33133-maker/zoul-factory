## About Spartifexon / 关于 Spartifexon

**Spartifexon** is a global OEM & ODM manufacturer with **17 years** of
expertise in consumer electronics. From rapid charging and high-speed cabling
to AI-powered eyewear and in-car connectivity, we deliver factory-direct,
brand-grade products to partners worldwide.

**Spartifexon** 是一家拥有 **17 年** 行业经验的全球消费电子 OEM & ODM 制造商，
专注于快充电源、高速数据线、AI 智能眼镜与车载互联产品，为全球品牌客户提供
工厂直供、品牌级品质的产品与服务。
