## Company Profile

Spartifexon is a global OEM & ODM manufacturer with **17 years** of heritage
in consumer electronics. The parent company was founded in **2008**, and in
**2014** Spartifexon established its dedicated smart wearable division —
today one of the longest-running specialised teams in the industry.

The smart wearable division integrates design, R&D, assembly, sales and
after-sales into a single in-house pipeline, delivering one-stop OEM & ODM
service to brand owners around the world. Spartifexon now employs more than
**500 staff**, of whom around **100 are R&D engineers**, and ships to more
than **80 countries** across Europe, the Americas, Australia, Asia-Pacific
and Africa.

## R&D Strength

Spartifexon's R&D group of 100+ specialists covers the full product stack:

- ID and UI design
- PCBA layout and hardware engineering
- Embedded firmware
- iOS and Android companion apps
- Cloud server and back-end services
- Test engineering and reliability validation

The team is dedicated to algorithmic innovation in smart-watch sensing and
to mature, repeatable execution of OEM & ODM programs at scale.

## Global Supply Chain

Spartifexon owns and operates assembly factories in **China** and **India**,
including a dedicated SMT plant. Dual-region manufacturing is the foundation
of our global supply chain — it shortens lead times, balances tariff
exposure, and keeps capacity resilient for partners shipping into multiple
markets.

- **China** — 500K units / month
- **India** — 300K units / month
- **Total monthly capacity** — 800K+ units

## Brand Story

**Innovation · Excellence · Cooperation · Win-Win.**

Spartifexon is a high-tech enterprise integrating R&D, production and sales
of smart wearable products, capable of providing one-stop OEM/ODM service
to customers in the global smart wearable industry.

Our self-developed and mass-produced series of smart bracelets, smart
watches, smart glasses, smart rings and Bluetooth earphones are well
received by leading brands worldwide for their excellent quality, exquisite
craftsmanship and outstanding cost-effectiveness.

For more than ten years Spartifexon has held to a single development
strategy: technological innovation. The company continues to push and break
through on the core algorithms of the smart wearable field, helping global
partners realise greater brand business value.
