# History / 发展历程

A 17-year journey — from a consumer-electronics start-up to a Top 3 global
OEM & ODM partner for the smart wearable industry.

17 年的发展之路——从消费电子创业团队，到全球前三的智能穿戴 OEM & ODM
合作伙伴。

---

## 2008 — Spartifexon Founded

The Spartifexon parent company is established, focusing on consumer
electronics design and manufacturing. The team lays the engineering and
supply-chain foundation that will later support the smart wearable line.

**2008 — Spartifexon 创立**

Spartifexon 母公司成立，聚焦消费电子产品的设计与制造，奠定了日后智能
穿戴产品线所依托的工程与供应链基础。

---

## 2014 — Smart Wearable Division Established

Spartifexon launches a dedicated smart wearable business unit, with R&D,
APP development and sales teams from day one. We become one of the
earliest companies in China to research, develop and manufacture smart
wearable technology end-to-end.

**2014 — 智能穿戴事业部成立**

Spartifexon 正式成立智能穿戴事业部，同步组建研发团队、APP 团队与销售
团队，是国内最早全链路自研、自产智能穿戴技术的公司之一。

---

## 2015 — First Owned Factory · ISO9001 & BSCI Certified

Spartifexon establishes its first wholly-owned assembly factory of more
than 2,000 m². The facility passes ISO9001 quality-management certification
and BSCI social-compliance audit in the same year.

**2015 — 自建工厂落成 · 通过 ISO9001 与 BSCI 认证**

Spartifexon 建立自有组装厂，占地面积达 2000 多平方米，并于同年通过
ISO9001 质量管理体系与 BSCI 社会责任认证。

---

## 2018 — GPS Innovation · Recognised as a High-Tech Enterprise

Spartifexon releases the F2, R3, R7 and M-series GPS watches and enters
strategic partnerships with major brands including Cellularline, boAt,
Noise, Timex and Polaroid. In the same year, Spartifexon is officially
recognised as a National **High-Tech Enterprise**.

**2018 — GPS 创新 · 荣获高新技术企业**

Spartifexon 推出 F2、R3、R7、M 系列 GPS 手表，并与 Cellularline、boAt、
Noise、Timex、Polaroid 等头部品牌客户达成战略合作。同年，Spartifexon
荣获"国家高新技术企业"认定。

---

## 2021 — 600K Monthly Capacity

To meet rapidly growing demand, Spartifexon expands assembly to 4,000+ m²
across 7 production lines, and stands up its own SMT plant of 2,000+ m²
with 4 SMT lines. The site is staffed by 200+ workers and a 70+ engineer
team, lifting monthly output to 400K-600K units.

**2021 — 月产能突破 60 万只**

为满足持续增长的产能需求，Spartifexon 把组装产线扩展到 4000 多平方米、
7 条生产线，并自建 2000 多平方米、4 条 SMT 产线的贴片厂；现场配备 200
多名工人与 70 多名工程师团队，月产能达到 40 万 – 60 万只。

---

## 2025 — Global Output Hits 800K Monthly · China + India

In February 2025 Spartifexon scales the manufacturing footprint to a
10,000 m² factory complex. Combined with assembly and SMT lines in India,
total monthly output reaches **500K in China and 300K in India** — a
global capacity of **800K+ units per month**.

**2025 — 全球月产量达 80 万只 · 中国 + 印度双基地**

2025 年 2 月，Spartifexon 把生产规模继续扩大到 10000 多平方米的厂房集群，
并在印度同步运行组装与 SMT 生产线。当前 **中国月产能 50 万只，印度月
产能 30 万只**，全球整体月产能突破 **80 万只**。
